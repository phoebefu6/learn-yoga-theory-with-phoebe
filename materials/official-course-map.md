# Official course map - Learn Yoga Theory with Phoebe

Maps the 9 theory sessions to the **Yoga Alliance RYS-200 core curriculum** (2020 Elevated Standards). This course teaches the **theory / knowledge** side of a 200-hour teacher training. The physical practicum (supervised teaching hours, live-adjusted asana practice, in-person contact hours) is NOT covered by design and must be done in an accredited in-person RYS - stated honestly on every page.

Verified facts + source-disagreement flags live in `research-canon.md` and `research-praxis.md`.

## RYS-200 core categories (verified)
| Category | Hours | This course |
|---|---|---|
| Techniques, Training & Practice | 75 | S4, S6, S7, S9 (theory of asana, pranayama, subtle body, meditation, sequencing) |
| Anatomy & Physiology | 30 | S5 (physical) + S4 (subtle) |
| Yoga Humanities (history, philosophy, ethics) | 30 | S1, S2, S3 |
| Professional Essentials (teaching methodology, prof dev, practicum) | 50 | S7, S8, S9 (methodology + prof dev; practicum is in-person only) |
| Flexible / elective | 15 | woven across |
Also required for RYT: a compulsory **equity in yoga** course (touched in S3 + S9; the full equity module is delivered by the registering school).

## The 80% bar
Each session teaches ~80% of the *working theory* of its mapped competency. What stays with an in-person RYS: supervised practicum teaching hours, hands-on physical adjustment practice, live asana refinement, and the school's own equity module + assessment. Said plainly on each page's "what this covers" row.

## Not covered by design (say it honestly)
- Supervised practicum / teaching-practice hours (in-person RYS only)
- Hands-on physical adjustment technique (must be learned live, with consent practice)
- Live asana practice + personal sadhana (this is theory, not a movement class)
- The registering school's equity-in-yoga module + final assessment / certification
- Medical / therapeutic yoga (separate advanced training)

## Honesty note
"Traditionally said to" framing for experiential/energetic claims (chakra effects, inversion thyroid benefit, twist detox) - taught as tradition, not clinical evidence. Dates for the texts are scholarly estimates (ranges, not fixed years). Sutra count 195/196 and Sun Salutation breath counts are edition/school dependent - flagged on-page.
