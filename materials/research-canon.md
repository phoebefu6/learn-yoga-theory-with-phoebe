# Research brief 1 - Classical yoga philosophy canon (verified)

Source domains noted per claim. Verse numbers + Sanskrit cross-checked, not invented. ⚠ = sources genuinely disagree (teach the disagreement).

## 1. Patanjali's Yoga Sutras

### 4 Padas (chapters)
| # | Pada | Covers | Sutras |
|---|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Samadhi Pada | contemplation; defines yoga, the vrittis, types of samadhi | 51 |
| 2 | Sadhana Pada | practice; kriya yoga, the kleshas, first 5 limbs | 55 |
| 3 | Vibhuti Pada | powers; last 3 limbs (samyama), the siddhis | 56 |
| 4 | Kaivalya Pada | liberation (kaivalya) through self-realization | 34 |

Total ~196 sutras. ⚠ commonly cited as 195 OR 196 depending on manuscript/commentary. Teach the range.

### 8 Limbs (Ashtanga, introduced Sutra 2.29)
1. Yama - ethical restraints (outer world)
2. Niyama - observances (self)
3. Asana - posture (steady, comfortable seat)
4. Pranayama - breath regulation / extension of life-force
5. Pratyahara - withdrawal of the senses
6. Dharana - concentration (single point)
7. Dhyana - meditation (uninterrupted flow)
8. Samadhi - absorption / union (the goal)

### 5 Yamas
Ahimsa (non-harming) · Satya (truthfulness) · Asteya (non-stealing) · Brahmacharya (⚠ classical = celibacy/chastity; modern YTT = moderation / right use of vital energy - teach both) · Aparigraha (non-possessiveness / non-greed)

### 5 Niyamas
Saucha (cleanliness/purity) · Santosha (contentment) · Tapas (discipline/self-heat) · Svadhyaya (self-study) · Ishvara Pranidhana (surrender to a higher power)

### Key sutras (numbering verified)
- **1.2 - Yogash chitta vritti nirodhah** - "Yoga is the stilling of the fluctuations (vrittis) of the mind (chitta)."
- **1.33 - four locks & keys**: maitri (friendliness) toward the happy, karuna (compassion) toward the suffering, mudita (joy) toward the virtuous, upeksha (equanimity) toward the non-virtuous - steadies the mind.
- **2.3 - the 5 Kleshas (afflictions)**: avidya (ignorance, the root) · asmita (egoism) · raga (attachment) · dvesha (aversion) · abhinivesha (fear of death/clinging to life).
- **2.46 - Sthira sukham asanam** - "Posture should be steady (sthira) and comfortable (sukha)." su=good, kha=space.

## 2. Bhagavad Gita (as used in YTT)
Paths: **Karma** (selfless action, nishkama karma) · **Bhakti** (loving devotion) · **Jnana** (knowledge/inquiry). ⚠ **Raja** yoga often added as a 4th "royal path" (meditation, Patanjali) - a later four-yogas synthesis (Vivekananda), NOT native to the Gita's three.
Concepts: dharma (duty/righteous action); Arjuna/Krishna dialogue on the Kurukshetra battlefield (Krishna as charioteer; part of the Mahabharata, 700 verses); action without attachment to fruits.

## 3. Hatha Yoga Pradipika
15th-c CE manual by Yogi Svatmarama; one of 3 classic hatha texts (+ Gheranda Samhita, Shiva Samhita). ~389 shlokas (⚠ varies by edition). 4 chapters: 1 Asana + diet, 2 Pranayama + shatkarma (6 purifications) + kumbhaka, 3 Mudra & Bandha + kundalini, 4 Samadhi + nada. A subtle-body system: nadis, prana, kundalini, chakras, bandhas. ha=sun, tha=moon (balancing pingala/ida).

## 4. Subtle body / energy anatomy

### 5 Koshas (outer -> inner, from Taittiriya Upanishad)
Annamaya (food/physical) · Pranamaya (energy/breath) · Manomaya (mental-emotional) · Vijnanamaya (wisdom/intellect) · Anandamaya (bliss).

### 3 Gunas
Sattva (clarity/balance) · Rajas (activity/passion) · Tamas (inertia/heaviness).

### 7 Chakras (order verified)
| # | Sanskrit | English | Location | Element | Theme |
|---|----------|---------|----------|---------|-------|
| 1 | Muladhara | Root | base of spine | Earth | grounding |
| 2 | Svadhisthana | Sacral | below navel/pelvis | Water | creativity |
| 3 | Manipura | Solar plexus | upper abdomen | Fire | power |
| 4 | Anahata | Heart | center of chest | Air | love |
| 5 | Vishuddha | Throat | throat | Ether/space | expression |
| 6 | Ajna | Third eye | between eyebrows | ⚠ beyond gross elements | intuition |
| 7 | Sahasrara | Crown | crown of head | ⚠ beyond gross elements | consciousness |
⚠ Only the lower 5 map to gross elements in classical tantra; 6-7 vary by source (popular charts say light/thought). Teach lower 5 as canonical, 6-7 as "beyond the elements."

### Nadis
Ida (left, cooling/lunar) · Pingala (right, heating/solar) · Sushumna (central; kundalini rises here).

### Prana + 5 Vayus
Prana (chest, intake/inhalation) · Apana (pelvis, elimination/downward) · Samana (navel, digestion/assimilation) · Udana (throat/head, upward, speech, exhalation) · Vyana (whole body, circulation).

### 3 Bandhas
Mula (root, pelvic floor) · Uddiyana (abdominal, upward lift) · Jalandhara (throat/chin). All three = Maha Bandha.

### Ayurveda - 3 Doshas
Vata (space+air, movement/nervous system) · Pitta (fire+water, digestion/metabolism) · Kapha (water+earth, structure/stability).

## 5. History / lineage (dates are scholarly estimates - present as approximate)
- Vedas ~1500-800 BCE · Upanishads ~1200-500 BCE (Taittiriya = source of koshas)
- Bhagavad Gita ~2nd c BCE - 2nd c CE (in Mahabharata)
- Yoga Sutras ~100 BCE-500 CE; ~400 CE common midpoint (⚠ do not assert one year)
- Hatha Yoga Pradipika ~15th c CE
- Modern postural yoga: early-mid 20th c, **Tirumalai Krishnamacharya** (1888-1989, "father of modern yoga," Mysore Palace).

### Krishnamacharya's students -> styles
B.K.S. Iyengar -> Iyengar (alignment, props) · K. Pattabhi Jois -> Ashtanga Vinyasa (dynamic set sequences; parent of Vinyasa/Power) · T.K.V. Desikachar (son) -> Viniyoga (therapeutic, individualized) · Indra Devi (spread yoga to the West). Most Western postural styles trace to the Mysore lineage.

## Flags to carry into the course
1. Sutra count 195 vs 196 (edition). 2. Yoga Sutras dating range, not one year. 3. Brahmacharya celibacy vs moderation. 4. Ajna/Sahasrara elements. 5. Raja as a "4th path" = later synthesis. 6. HYP verse count ~389 varies.
