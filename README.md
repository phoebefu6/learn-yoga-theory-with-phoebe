# Learn Yoga Theory with Phoebe

The **theory** half of a 200-hour yoga teacher training, in nine focused sessions - mapped to the Yoga Alliance RYS-200 standard. Philosophy, the eight limbs, the subtle and physical body, asana logic, sequencing, and the craft of teaching, taught with inline diagrams that make the Sanskrit stick.

## What this is - and isn't

This teaches the **knowledge** a 200-hour training expects, so you walk into your program already fluent. It does **not** certify you to teach: the practicum (supervised teaching hours, live physical adjustments, your own on-the-mat practice) and the certificate itself require an accredited in-person Registered Yoga School (RYS). Experiential and energetic claims are taught as tradition ("traditionally said to"), not clinical fact; text dates and verse counts are given as ranges, not false precision.
