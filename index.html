Learn Yoga Theory with Phoebe

The theory of a 200-hour yoga training, in nine sessions

Everything a 200-hour teacher training expects you to KNOW - philosophy, the eight limbs, the subtle and physical body, asana logic, sequencing, and the craft of teaching - mapped to the Yoga Alliance standard, easy to hard, with diagrams that make the Sanskrit stick.

🌱 Session 1 · easiest

Roots & lineage: what yoga actually is

Yoga means union, not stretching. The timeline from the Vedas to your studio, the lineage behind the styles, and the map of the whole course.

▶ Start hereHistory 🪜 Session 2 · building

The eight limbs: Patanjali's map

The Yoga Sutras, the eight-limbed path (ashtanga), the five yamas and niyamas, and the sutras every teacher can quote.

Philosophy 🕉️ Session 3 · building

The Gita & the paths of yoga

Karma, bhakti and jnana yoga, dharma, acting without attachment, and the ethics a teacher carries - including equity in yoga.

Philosophy + ethics 🔥 Session 4 · deepening

The subtle body: koshas, chakras & prana

The energy anatomy of yoga: five koshas, three gunas, seven chakras, the nadis, the five vayus, the bandhas, and Ayurveda's doshas.

Subtle anatomy 🦴 Session 5 · deepening

Functional anatomy for yoga

Bones, planes and the spine; the muscles that matter; why the breath calms the nervous system; and how to keep students safe.

Anatomy & physiology 🧘 Session 6 · deepening

Asana theory: the logic of posture

Steady and comfortable (sthira sukham asanam), the families of posture, alignment principles, reading Sanskrit pose names, props and counterposes.

Techniques 🌊 Session 7 · advanced

Course flow theory: sequencing a class

The arc of a class, vinyasa krama and the peak-pose method, sun salutations A and B, class types, and the energetic rise-and-settle.

Sequencing 🗣️ Session 8 · advanced

Cueing & analogy theory: teaching craft

The four cueing channels, why imagery beats anatomy, invitational language, and hands-on assists done with real consent.

Teaching methodology 🌬️ Session 9 · advanced

Pranayama, meditation & becoming a teacher

Breath techniques, the inner limbs of meditation, scope and ethics, and your concrete path from theory to a registered 200-hour teacher.

Professional essentials
easiest - start here building - philosophy deepening - the body advanced - teaching craft
Theory, not certification. This course teaches the knowledge half of a 200-hour training so you walk into your program already fluent. The practicum - supervised teaching hours, live physical adjustments, your own on-the-mat practice, and the certificate itself - can only happen in an accredited in-person Registered Yoga School (RYS).
